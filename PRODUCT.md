# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wallet users monitor and manage their Zcash funds from a terminal. During sync, they need a quick sense of where work is happening on the chain without interpreting a diagnostic trace.

## Product Purpose

Zingolib provides the wallet library and terminal interface behind Zcash wallet operations. The sync view should make current work legible while keeping the wallet task in focus.

## Brand Personality

Calm, operational, and precise.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboards that make every metric equally prominent, decorative animation, and verbose diagnostic labels in the main view.

## Design Principles

- Give the current task visual priority.
- Show live work as a compact spatial cue.
- Keep background detail quiet until it helps a decision.
- Make sync state readable at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The main sync timeline may use colour and visual weight without duplicating priority as text. Existing terminal themes and monochrome support remain intact.
